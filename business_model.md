# Menu Man — Business Model

## Product

Menu Man provides managed websites and digital menu infrastructure
specifically for independent restaurants.

The primary product is not simply "a website."
Menu Man manages the restaurant's customer-facing digital storefront.

## Target Customer

Independent/local restaurants that:

- Have no dedicated website, or have a poor/outdated one
- Depend heavily on DoorDash, Grubhub, Toast, Google, etc.
- Have messy, duplicated, incomplete, or poorly presented online menus
- Do not have staff dedicated to website/menu management
- Want a professional online presence without maintaining it themselves

Initial emphasis: casual restaurants with meaningful menu complexity.

## Core Value Proposition

Give customers one clean, fast, branded place to:

- Understand what the restaurant is
- Browse and search the full menu
- See food photography
- Find prices, hours, address and contact information
- Choose pickup or delivery
- Reach the restaurant's existing ordering provider
- Access ancillary functions such as reservations, catering or gift cards

Menu Man handles the maintenance.

## Differentiation

Restaurant-specialized rather than generic website building.

Potential differentiators:

- Structured menu database
- Excellent menu search
- Menu/category navigation
- Large-menu usability
- Food imagery
- Mobile-first restaurant UX
- Menu cleanup and normalization
- Managed menu updates
- Multiple ordering destinations
- Automatically generated printable/PDF menu
- Restaurant-specific analytics/reporting
- Restaurant-specific page/component templates
- Potential future synchronization with third-party platforms

## Ordering Strategy

Do not initially become the restaurant's transaction processor.

Use the restaurant's existing provider where appropriate:

Menu Man -> Pickup -> Toast / Square / Clover / etc.
Menu Man -> Delivery -> DoorDash / Grubhub / etc.
Menu Man -> Reservations -> OpenTable / Resy / etc.
Menu Man -> Gift Cards -> existing gift-card provider

Menu Man owns the discovery and presentation layer.
Existing vendors retain transaction and fulfillment responsibilities.

## Pricing — Working Hypothesis

Base managed plan target: $99/month

One-time buildout/setup fee: TBD

Possible add-ons: TBD

Do not compete solely on website creation price.
UENI and similar generalized providers can produce basic websites
for far less.

$99/month must be justified by restaurant specialization,
ongoing management, menu infrastructure, and measurable utility.

## Competitive Reference

UENI:
Website Launch — $24.99/mo + $79 setup
Plus — $59/mo + $79 setup
Ecommerce — $99/mo + $79 setup

Lesson:
Generic website presence is commoditized.

Menu Man must provide materially more restaurant-specific value.

## Sales Channel

Initial approach likely direct local outreach.

Ideal prospects can be identified through:
Google Maps
restaurants without websites
restaurants with poor websites
restaurants using only social media / delivery marketplace pages
restaurants with particularly messy third-party menus

Demo/reference restaurant: Armando's Mexican Food.

## Sales Story

Avoid:
"You need a nicer website."

Prefer:
"Your customers are currently being sent through a fragmented,
poorly presented combination of Google, DoorDash and social media.
We give them one professional place to find everything and keep it
updated for you."
