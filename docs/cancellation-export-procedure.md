# Manual cancellation and information export

Owner-approved policy for Marketing V1.2. This is a manual procedure; there is no cancellation, retention, deletion, or restoration automation introduced by this release.

1. Receive the request at hello@getmenuman.com. Verify the requester is authorized for the restaurant before changing service or sharing information. Record the request, confirmed cancellation date, current paid-through date, and the customer's choice: immediate website shutdown or service through the paid-through date. Confirm these dates in writing. Cancel future renewal using the existing billing process; do not perform payment changes from the marketing site.
2. Explain that setup and subscription payments are nonrefundable and there are no prorated refunds for unused subscription time, except where required by law. Escalate any legally required exception to the owner. The standard model is $300 setup at project start, no monthly charges while building, then $99/month from completed launch with monthly anniversary renewals. Use verified billing records for dates; do not guess or promise a refund.
3. Create or verify a usable standard export of the restaurant-owned raw menu and business information. Record its private storage location, responsible person, cancellation date, and the date 90 calendar days after cancellation. Keep the information needed to honor requests available through that date. Set manual reminders and a backup responsible person. Do not put customer exports, credentials, contact records, or private files in Git or public asset folders.
4. Accept export requests received within the 90-day window, even if fulfillment takes longer. Verify the requester, review the export for other tenants' information and secrets, and deliver through an agreed private channel. Record the request and delivery dates; preserve a pending request until it is resolved. Do not promise source-code ownership, free custom transfers, or a particular restoration result.
5. After the window, the owner reviews preservation or disposal under applicable obligations and existing backup practices. The 90-day request window is not an automatic deletion deadline. Website design or functionality may remain longer, but availability and restoration are not guaranteed. Quote any reactivation or setup fees before work if the customer returns.

Before publishing, assign the primary and backup operators and confirm the private export location, reminders, shutdown process, and billing cancellation process can honor this policy. None of those actions were performed by this release task.
