No other item received an image. Existing protected photos of generic sandwiches, meat combinations, sides or sweets were not assigned to a specific menu item without a clear identity match. The scraper has no saved Upper Crust result; its one public attempt reached a challenge page after its automation-evasion flag was removed. No attempt was made to proceed past that page. Scraped DoorDash image URLs and downloaded files were not used.
